Biography

My name is Peace Ogunlana. I have a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics from the University of Ibadan. I am a teacher with a strong interest in technology, problem-solving and continuous learning.

My background in Mathematics has developed my analytical and logical thinking skills. As a teacher, I have also developed strong communication, organisation and problem-solving abilities.

I have always been fascinated by technology and its ability to transform ideas into practical solutions. This interest encouraged me to begin my journey into software engineering and full-stack development.
